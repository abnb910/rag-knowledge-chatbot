## Leave policy

Full-time employees get 25 days of annual leave per year, plus public holidays.
Leave requests need manager approval at least 2 weeks in advance for blocks longer than 5 days.
Unused leave up to 5 days can be carried into the next calendar year.

## Expense policy

Expenses under 500 GBP can be approved by your line manager.
Expenses of 500 GBP or more need Finance approval as well.
Submit claims within 30 days via the expenses portal, with receipts attached.

## IT support

For laptop issues, contact the IT helpdesk at it-help@example.com.
Standard laptop refresh cycle is 3 years.
VPN is required when accessing company systems from outside the office.
